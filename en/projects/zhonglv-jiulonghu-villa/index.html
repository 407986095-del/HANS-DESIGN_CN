VISUAL READING

Let the courtyard become another side of the living room.

An editorial reading of the available images; it does not state project responsibilities or construction history.

01 / The question

How can a room retain its enclosure while opening its view to a courtyard? These images offer a starting point for reading that relationship.

02 / The observation

Broad glazing brings planting and the stone wall into view. Low seating lets the eye travel across the furniture toward the courtyard.

03 / The detail

Pale flooring, timber and stone establish a dialogue of tone and texture. Notice how daylight reveals both the solidity of stone and the softness of fabric.
