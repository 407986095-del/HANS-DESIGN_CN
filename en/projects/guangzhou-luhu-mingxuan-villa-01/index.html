VISUAL READING

Find an everyday scale within a tall room.

An editorial reading of the available images; it does not state project responsibilities or construction history.

01 / The question

How can the openness of a tall room coexist with the intimacy of gathering? Look at the contrast between its high and low elements.

02 / The observation

Tall windows emphasize height, while seating and tables bring the centre of activity close to the floor.

03 / The detail

Suspended lighting bridges the vertical space, with planting beyond the window as a soft backdrop. Follow the relationship between window, light and furniture.
