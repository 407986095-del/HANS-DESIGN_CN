Read the depth of a room through its stair.

An editorial reading of the available images; it does not state project responsibilities or construction history.

01 / The question

How do living spaces connect to another level without an abrupt break? The stair becomes a way of reading depth.

02 / The observation

The view moves from the foreground seating and table to the stair beyond. Horizontal furniture lines reinforce that sequence.

Alternating material tones and localized lighting produce a restrained rhythm. Look more closely at the meeting of handrail, tread and wall.
