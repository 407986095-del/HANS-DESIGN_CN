VISUAL READING

Make the dining table the centre of gathering.

An editorial reading of the available images; it does not state project responsibilities or construction history.

01 / The question

How can different activities find their own centre in an open dining space? Begin with the relationship of table and island.

02 / The observation

The dining table anchors the composition, with the island and cabinetry defining its side and rear edges.

03 / The detail

Timber, pale stone and slender lighting establish a contrast of visual weight. Local lighting adds a second layer around objects and surfaces.
