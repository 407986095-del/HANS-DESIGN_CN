IMAGE-LED READING

Read the sequence of a multi-level residence through its entrance and stair.

This reading separates what is directly visible from interpretive design clues. Year, area and completion status are not inferred from the images.

01 / Visible in the images

The design renderings show the courtyard, entrance stair, shared living areas, tea room, dining room, kitchen and bedrooms; the principal confirms the project was completed and handed over for use.

02 / Design reading

The entrance and stair offer a starting point for reading the interior, followed by material and sightline relationships among shared rooms and a quieter expression toward the bedrooms. This is an interpretation of the renderings, not a claim that every built detail matches them.
