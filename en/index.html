A WAY OF SEEING

Before style, read the life inside.

A private home asks for more than a striking image. It asks for connected decisions about scale, circulation, storage, material, light and implementation.

Light

Notice where daylight enters, where it pauses, and how it changes through the day.

Movement

Understand how private and shared routines meet, and where their paths should remain separate.

Time

Plan for a home that can remain calm as people, objects and habits change.

SERVICE AREA

Residential design in Guangdong.

The current service scope covers flats, villas and detached homes in Guangdong. Design deliverables and any construction follow-up are defined for each project after an initial conversation; on-site follow-up is for projects in Guangdong.
